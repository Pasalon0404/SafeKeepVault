Keep documents in your vault: a letter of instruction, a will, recovery steps or scans, brought in from the Transfer Drive and read on SafeKeep itself. Plus a vault three times larger on newly flashed drives, and Paste in Encrypted Notes without a prompt. Earlier changes are in the [v1.67](https://github.com/Pasalon0404/SafeKeepVault/releases/tag/v1.67) release notes.

### 📄 Documents
In **Encrypted Notes → Documents**, next to your notes.
- **Import from Transfer Drive:** choose a file on the Transfer Drive and it's saved in the encrypted vault, checked byte for byte. **Remove it from the Transfer Drive after importing** is checked by default, because the Transfer Drive isn't encrypted (and removing a file there isn't a secure erase).
- **Shown in SafeKeep:** PDFs (page by page, with zoom), JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP images, text files (`.txt`, `.md`, `.csv`) and OpenDocument text (`.odt`, as a reading view: headings, lists, tables, images and footnotes, not the exact page layout).
- **Stored and exported:** any other file, such as Word documents or videos. **Export** copies a document back to the Transfer Drive, with a reminder that the copy there isn't encrypted.
- **Rename** and **Delete** on each document. Up to 100 MB each; the tab shows how much room the vault has left.
- **Encrypted Backup** has a new **Documents** item. Restore shows how many documents a backup holds. Opened with 7-Zip on another computer, the backup's `documents` folder has a `README - document names.txt` listing which file is which.
- **Safe to open:** documents are shown in a sealed viewer that can't read your vault or reach the rest of SafeKeep, even if a file were made to attack it. PDFs are drawn by pdf.js, never by the browser's own PDF viewer, and nothing in a document can run.
- Not available in a Temporary Session, since documents live in the vault.

Tested on a flash drive with this release's code: PDFs, images, text and `.odt` files, and an Encrypted Backup with Documents opened on a Mac.

### 🔐 A larger vault on new flash drives
A newly flashed drive now has a **900 MB** vault partition, about 750 MB usable, up from 300 MB. It still fits the smallest 8 GB flash drives. First setup takes a minute or two longer while the vault is created.

Updating in place keeps your current vault, so a drive updated to v1.68 keeps its 300 MB vault (Documents works there too, with less room). To move to the larger vault: make an Encrypted Backup that includes everything you keep, including Documents, flash a new drive with this release, then restore the backup.

### 📋 Paste in Encrypted Notes
**Paste** in the note editor works at once. Before, the browser asked for permission to read the clipboard each time.

### 🎲 Entropy Generator
The result shows your seed words first. The hex and binary are under **Show technical details (hex and binary)**.

### 🛠️ For people who build SafeKeep themselves
The USB image now also builds in an Ubuntu virtual machine on an Apple Silicon Mac. See "On an Apple Silicon Mac" in the README.

### 🔄 Update in place from v1.47 or later
1. Copy `SafeKeep-Update-v1.68.tar` onto your flash drive's TRANSFER drive.
2. Boot SafeKeep and unlock your vault.
3. Open Device Options → Update SafeKeep → Check for update, then Install and Restart now.

Your vault, how it unlocks and your Transfer Drive are not touched (the vault keeps its size). Before installing, SafeKeep shows the app's SHA-256: it should match the `boot.html` line of this release's `SHA256SUMS`.

1. Download `SafeKeep-Release-v1.68.zip`, `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `SHA256SUMS.txt.asc`.
