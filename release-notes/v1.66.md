More you can do with a Trezor One without Trezor Suite: sign with its passphrase wallet, sign single-signature transactions, turn passphrase protection on or off, erase it, and set it up from recovery words, with a blank grid so the computer never sees the words. Earlier changes are in the [v1.65](https://github.com/Pasalon0404/SafeKeepVault/releases/tag/v1.65) release notes.

### 🔑 Trezor passphrase wallets
In the **PSBT Signer**, check **Use a passphrase on the Trezor** under the Trezor button.
- Type the passphrase twice, choose it from the **Passphrase Library**, or use the one applied on the Dashboard.
- SafeKeep checks that the passphrase opens a wallet whose key is in the transaction before anything is sent to sign, and forgets it once the Trezor has signed.
- Refused if the Trezor's passphrase protection is off, since it would otherwise sign with its standard wallet.
- Works when SafeKeep holds no seed too (a Temporary Session, or a vault that doesn't keep the seed).

### ✍️ Single-signature hardware signing
The co-signing card used to appear only for multisig. A **Trezor** (with or without a passphrase) or a **Coldcard** can now sign a single-signature transaction from its own seed too, in a normal session or with **Sign with Hardware Wallet** in a Temporary Session.

### 🛠️ Trezor settings without Trezor Suite
In **Device Options → Trezor** (in a Temporary Session, the **Trezor Tools** button on the welcome screen). The Trezor asks you to confirm each change on its own screen.
- **Passphrase Protection:** on or off.
- **Erase This Trezor:** check a box and type ERASE. SafeKeep erases only the Trezor it unlocked, and removes its pairing afterwards.
- **Recover from Words:** sets up an erased Trezor from 12, 18 or 24 words, with a new PIN.
  - **From a paper backup:** advanced recovery. For each word the Trezor shows a grid on its own screen, and you click the same spot on SafeKeep's blank grid, 4 clicks per word. Only the spot reaches the computer, never the word. **Back One Step** (or Backspace) undoes a click, and the number keys work too.
  - **From SafeKeep's Seed Vault:** advanced recovery with SafeKeep showing each word, or **Quicker: click the words**. For 12 or 18 words the Trezor first asks you to relax its safety checks; unplug it afterwards to put them back to Strict.

Recover from Words was tested on a Trezor One.

### 🐞 Fix
- **Sending a passphrase to a COLDCARD:** the Transfer Drive's file sorting could move a **Send a Passphrase to the Coldcard** request into the Passphrase Library before the COLDCARD received it. It no longer touches requests meant for a COLDCARD or Trezor.

### 🔄 Update in place from v1.47 or later
1. Copy `SafeKeep-Update-v1.66.tar` onto your flash drive's TRANSFER drive.
2. Boot SafeKeep and unlock your vault.
3. Open Device Options → Update SafeKeep → Check for update, then Install and Restart now.

Your vault, your vault password and your Transfer Drive are not touched. Before installing, SafeKeep shows the app's SHA-256: it should match the `boot.html` line of this release's `SHA256SUMS`.
