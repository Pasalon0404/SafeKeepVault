Security fixes from a full review of SafeKeep, a Transfer Drive that fills larger flash drives, and a Saved Wallets tab in Output Descriptor. Earlier changes are in the [v1.68](https://github.com/Pasalon0404/SafeKeepVault/releases/tag/v1.68) release notes.

### 🛡️ Security fixes
We reviewed every way someone could reach the vault: networking, Bluetooth and other radios, disks, USB and other ports, backups and updates, and the cryptography in the app. There is still no network or radio path into SafeKeep, and the review found no errors in its cryptography. It did find these gaps, now closed:
- **Restore brings back your data only.** Restoring an Encrypted Backup copied everything in the backup into the vault. A backup someone had tampered with could include files that SafeKeep treats as requests, such as a factory reset. Restore now copies only your notes, passphrases, 2FA codes, settings, documents and master seed, and skips anything else. Backups you made yourself always restored correctly and still do.
- **The computer no longer sleeps.** Closing the lid or pressing a sleep key now does nothing, so **Auto-Lock** and **Auto-Off** (Device Options) keep counting while the lid is closed. Before, a laptop put to sleep kept the vault unlocked, and the timers paused until it woke. If a computer sleeps anyway, SafeKeep locks on waking once the Auto-Lock time has passed.
- **Locked after an unexpected restart.** If SafeKeep's window ever closes unexpectedly and restarts, it now locks the vault and asks for your passphrase again, instead of reopening it.
- **No swap at startup.** SafeKeep turned off swap before the unlock prompt, so your vault was never written to a swap partition. It now also makes sure swap is never turned on during startup, even if a flash drive with a swap partition is plugged in.
- **FireWire turned off.** A FireWire device can read a computer's memory directly, so SafeKeep no longer loads FireWire drivers. Nothing in SafeKeep uses FireWire.
- **Smaller changes:** the backup's file name is checked before restoring, and a backup that is opened but not restored is cleared from memory after 10 minutes.

### 🔑 Six-word passphrases by default
The BIP-39 Passphrase Generator now starts at **6 words** (66 bits), up from 4 words (44 bits). 4 and 8 words are still available, and the strength meter shows the bits for each.

### 💾 A Transfer Drive that fills your flash drive
SafeKeep's image is made for 8 GB flash drives, so on a larger drive most of the space went unused. Now, at **first setup** on a newly flashed drive larger than 8 GB (16, 32 or 64 GB, for example), the Transfer Drive grows to fill the rest of the drive. A Mac or PC then shows one large TRANSFER drive. The vault stays 900 MB.

This happens only while the Transfer Drive is still empty. If you copied an Encrypted Backup onto it before first setup, it is left as it is. Drives that are already set up, or that update in place, keep their current Transfer Drive. Tested on a larger flash drive with a Mac; not yet tested with Windows.

### 👛 Saved Wallets in Output Descriptor
**Output Descriptor → Saved Wallets** is now its own tab. Each saved wallet can be renamed, and opening one shows **How this wallet is built**: its script type, how many keys must sign, and each key's fingerprint, derivation path and extended public key, with the key from this seed marked.

### 🌙 Dark mode
Drop-down lists, such as choosing a seed to send to a Coldcard, are now readable in dark mode.

### 🛠️ For people who build SafeKeep themselves
- The app's source folder is now `app/` (it was `seed-xor-tool/`). Build it with `cd app && npm ci && npm run build`. `verify-release` still rebuilds earlier releases from their old folder.
- `build.sh` now looks inside the finished startup image. It stops if the image contains a network or Bluetooth driver or could turn on swap, or if the firewall isn't installed and blocking everything.

### 🔄 Update in place from v1.47 or later
1. Copy `SafeKeep-Update-v1.69.tar` onto your flash drive's TRANSFER drive.
2. Boot SafeKeep and unlock your vault.
3. Open Device Options → Update SafeKeep → Check for update, then Install and Restart now.

All of the security fixes come with the update. Your vault, how it unlocks and your Transfer Drive are not touched; the Transfer Drive keeps its size. Before installing, SafeKeep shows the app's SHA-256: it should match the `boot.html` line of this release's `SHA256SUMS`.
